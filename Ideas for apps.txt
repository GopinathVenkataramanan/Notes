Fast Build: A "Checklist & Filing" app for one specific regulation. Use a simple form-based UI that generates the required PDF report.

Global Audience: Target one regulation that affects a large group (e.g., GDPR for US-based indie hackers).

Real Revenue: $29–$99/year per business. It’s an "insurance" purchase—they pay to not get fined.

Global Audience: Solopreneurs and creators globally.
