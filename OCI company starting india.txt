# **Step-by-Step Process for OCI to Register a Private Limited Company in India**

---

## **Step 1: Check FDI Sector Compliance**

Since OCIs are considered **foreign investors under FEMA**, first confirm:

1. Your business sector falls under **automatic route** or **government approval route**.

   * Automatic route examples: IT services, trading (non-defense), consultancy, e-commerce (non-retail), software.
   * Government approval examples: Defense, telecom, print media, aviation.

> If government approval is needed, apply to **Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT)** or respective ministry **before incorporation**.

---

## **Step 2: Obtain Digital Signature Certificate (DSC)**

**Purpose:** For signing MCA online forms.

* **OCI holders can obtain DSC**.
* Submit:

  * Passport (OCI card)
  * Recent photograph
  * Email and mobile number
* Cost: ₹1,500–3,000 per DSC.
* Valid for 1–2 years.

> Note: If you have Indian resident directors, their DSC can also be used.

---

## **Step 3: Apply for Director Identification Number (DIN)**

* Every director (including OCI) needs a DIN.
* Submit **DIR-3** form on MCA portal with:

  * Passport + OCI card
  * Address proof (foreign address allowed; some banks may ask for Indian address later)
* OCI holders are treated as **foreign directors**.

> Tip: DIN can be applied **simultaneously with SPICe+**.

---

## **Step 4: Name Approval (RUN Form)**

* Use MCA’s **RUN (Reserve Unique Name)** service.
* Submit up to 2 names with brief justification.
* Approval usually takes 1–2 days.

> OCI status does not restrict name reservation.

---

## **Step 5: Prepare Incorporation Documents**

1. **Memorandum of Association (MOA)** – Defines company’s objectives.
2. **Articles of Association (AOA)** – Defines management rules.
3. **Declaration & Affidavit by Directors/Subscribers** – Include OCI passport/OCI card details.
4. **Registered Office Proof** – Rent agreement + NOC from owner in India.

> Key Difference: OCI directors’ details (passport + OCI card) must be clearly mentioned.

---

## **Step 6: File Incorporation (SPICe+ Form)**

* Online submission on MCA portal.
* Covers:

  * Company name approval
  * DIN allotment
  * PAN & TAN application
* Attach: MOA, AOA, DSC, proof of registered office, director declarations.

**Fees:**

* Government fees: ₹7,000–10,000 (for authorized capital up to ₹1 lakh)
* Stamp duty: Varies by state

> OCI holders do not pay extra fees, but FDI reporting may apply later.

---

## **Step 7: Receive Certificate of Incorporation (COI)**

* MCA issues **COI** with:

  * Corporate Identity Number (CIN)
  * Company name
  * Registered office

> This legally creates your Pvt Ltd company.

---

## **Step 8: PAN & TAN**

* Automatically applied via SPICe+.
* OCI directors are treated as **foreign directors**, so provide passport & OCI card for PAN.

---

## **Step 9: Open Bank Account**

* Open a **current account in the company’s name**.
* Documents: COI, PAN, MOA, AOA, director IDs.
* Some banks may require at least **one resident Indian director** for practical reasons.

---

## **Step 10: FDI Compliance (Post-Incorporation)**

* Since OCI is a foreign investor, you must **report investment to RBI** using:

  * **Form FC-GPR** within **30 days of receiving investment**.
* If the sector requires approval, ensure **prior government approval** is obtained.

---

## **Step 11: Post-Incorporation Compliance**

1. **Maintain statutory records:**

   * Registers of directors, shareholders, minutes of meetings.
2. **Annual filings:**

   * Annual return (MGT-7)
   * Financial statements (AOC-4)
3. **Tax registrations if required:**

   * GST (if applicable)
   * Professional tax, Shops & Establishment license
4. **Other licenses:** Depending on business type (FSSAI, MSME, IEC, etc.).

---

## **Additional Notes for OCI Holders**

| Topic       | Note                                                                                    |
| ----------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Land        | Cannot buy **agricultural land**. Only non-agricultural property is allowed.            |
| Bank        | Some banks may ask for **Indian resident director**.                                    |
| Taxes       | Same as Indian resident companies. OCI is treated as foreign shareholder for reporting. |
| Investments | FDI rules apply; government approval needed for restricted sectors.                     |
