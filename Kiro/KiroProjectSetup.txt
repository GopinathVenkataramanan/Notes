If your priorities are:

1. **100% open-source stack**
2. **Scalable from MVP → production**
3. **Web + mobile sharing code**
4. **Docker-first deployment**
5. **No AWS lock-in**
6. **Can run on your own servers/VPS/Kubernetes later**

then I would adjust the TurboStarter-style architecture slightly.

The goal is a **cloud-agnostic, open-source SaaS platform architecture**.

Avoid AWS S3 dependency.

Use:
