# 1. The Combined Product Vision

Not:

* A finance tracker ❌
* A discipline app ❌

Instead:

> **A system that tracks your net worth and actively shapes your daily financial behavior to improve it**

Think:

* Mint → shows data
* YNAB → helps plan

You:

> **“We grow your net worth by controlling your behavior in real time.”**

---

# 2. How the Integration Actually Works

## Layer 1: Net Worth Engine (Awareness)

* Assets (cash, investments, property)
* Liabilities (loans, mortgage)
* Real-time net worth

---

## Layer 2: Intelligence Layer (Decision)

This is where most apps stop—but yours starts.

Example outputs:

* “You’re increasing net worth by $400/month”
* “At this rate, you’ll hit your goal in 6 years”
* “Dining spending is slowing your growth by 18%”

---

## Layer 3: Behavior Engine (Action)

This is your previous idea:

* Spending friction (delays, limits)
* Auto-saving rules
* Pre-commitment locks

---

## Layer 4: Feedback Loop (Addiction layer)

Every action shows impact:

> “Skipping that $30 spend increased your yearly net worth by $520”

This is where psychology kicks in using:

* Loss Aversion
* Present Bias

---

# 3. Your Core Differentiator (this is your moat)

Most apps:

You:

> “Here’s your money—and here’s how your next decision changes your future”

That’s a big shift.

# 4. Key Features (Integrated, not separate)

## A. Net Worth Impact per Action (killer feature)

Every expense shows:

* Immediate impact
* Long-term impact

Example:

> “$50 spent = -$1,200 future net worth (if invested instead)”

---

## B. Smart Spending Guardrails

Based on net worth goals:

* If user is behind → stricter controls
* If ahead → relaxed

Dynamic discipline.

---

## C. Goal-Driven Automation

User sets:

* “Reach $100K net worth”

System adjusts:

* Savings rate
* Spending limits

---

## D. Scenario + Enforcement combo

Not just:

* “What if you save more?”

But:

* “We’ll help you actually do it”

---

## E. “Net Worth Momentum” score

Show:

* Direction (up/down)
* Speed of growth

People love momentum metrics.
