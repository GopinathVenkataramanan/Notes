### Solution Architect Focus Areas

* Focus on **business process transformation**, not just technology replacement
* Challenge legacy customizations before replicating them
* Prioritize **integration and identity architecture**
* Define clear **system-of-record ownership**
* Design for **coexistence during transition**
* Ensure strong **vendor governance and SLA management**
* Drive user adoption through **change management early**

* Automate everything possible

* Plan for **rollback and operational support early**

### Core mindset for a Solution Architect

* Start with **business capability**, not technology
* Prefer **loosely coupled integrations**
* Design for **security + observability first**
* Keep platforms **cloud-agnostic where practical**
* Standardize through **APIs, events, and automation**
* Build for **operability and scalability from day one**
